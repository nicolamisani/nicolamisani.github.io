Simulations

LCA Higher or Lower

A game on the carbon footprint of everyday food and drink

Which is worse, wine or beer? Tofu or lentils? Each round shows two foods or drinks, and you pick the one with the higher carbon emissions per serving. The figures come from life cycle assessment (LCA): they cover the whole life of the product, from growing or rearing it to processing, packaging, transport, the shop, and keeping it at home.

I use it in my sustainability courses to show where the environmental impact of the products we use every day actually comes from, and how often our intuitions get it wrong.

You can also play a solo version at your own pace and test your knowledge.
